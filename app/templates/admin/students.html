Import roster

Paste whatever the study administration gave you — a CSV export, a column copied out of Excel, or a plain list of "Name <email>" lines. Anything with a recognisable email address per line is picked up; names are guessed from the email if none is found. Already-known emails are skipped, so pasting the same list twice is harmless.

Add one student

Same effect as pasting a single "Name <email>" line above — use whichever's quicker. Adding them here immediately mints their personal link, ready to send from the table below.

Deactivate hides someone from team dropdowns and shuffles without touching anything they've already handed in — the right choice for someone who's left the course mid-semester. Reactivate simply undoes that; it doesn't restore them to any team they were removed from in the meantime. Delete removes them from the list for good, along with any solo deliveries they own; deliveries they submitted on behalf of a team stay with the team, only the "submitted by" name clears. Editing a name or email below changes it everywhere — past deliveries, feedback emails, everything — it does not create a new student.
